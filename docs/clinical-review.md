# Outstanding Clinical Review

## Periodontal candidate-classification decision table

Clinical sign-off remains required for the
[periodontal classification decision table](specs/periodontal-classification-decision-table.md),
including:

- missing-data behavior;
- stage escalation, tooth-loss, and complexity precedence;
- direct and indirect grading evidence;
- risk modifiers and conflicting criteria; and
- confirmation, overrides, and the confirmed basis included in output.

The implementation and technical regression coverage exist. Until sign-off is
recorded, the decision table's existing clinical-review warning remains in
effect.

The previously proposed periodontal output-detail selector remains deferred.
Revisit it only through a separately scoped request after clinical review shows
a concrete need for multiple output modes.
