> Implemented and clinically accepted on 2026-08-27.

# CAMBRA123 2021 Caries Risk Assessment

- Date: 2026-08-27
- Status: Implemented for ages 0–6 and ages 6–adult
- Clinical acceptance: Accepted

## Decision

The 2026 Adult and Adolescent Hygiene encounter uses the January 2021
CAMBRA123 caries-risk assessment for ages 6 through adult. The Child Recare
Exam & Hygiene encounter provides an explicit choice between the published
ages 0–6 and ages 6–adult instruments. Age 6 overlaps both instruments and
remains a clinician choice; dentition state does not select or switch the
instrument.

The ages 0–6 assessment uses its own published items, scoring guidance, and
`Very High` category rather than reusing the ages 6–adult `Extreme` category.
Answers for both instruments are retained when the clinician switches age
bands, but only the selected instrument is included in the generated note.

## Current implementation

The ages 6–adult assessment in the shared 2026 Adult and Adolescent encounter
provides:

- the 8 protective factors in Column 1 at −1 each;
- the 8 biological/environmental risk factors in Column 2 at +2 each;
- the 4 disease indicators in Column 3 at +3 each;
- immediate score and category recalculation whenever a Yes checkbox is
  checked or unchecked;
- a periodontal-style collapsible factor-entry card whose header retains the
  Yes count, total score, and suggested or final category while the score,
  decision support, final category, and notes remain visible;
- the CAMBRA123 quantitative score and published score band;
- guideline flags for disease indicators and measured reduced salivary
  function;
- an explicit clinician-selected final category that is never changed
  automatically; and
- structured note output containing Yes responses, column totals, total score,
  final category, and notes.

The assessment belongs to the shared encounter, so it appears in Complete,
Hygiene, and Recare output and is available in both the 2026 Adult and 2026
Adolescent templates.

The ages 0–6 assessment in the Child Recare Exam & Hygiene encounter provides:

- 4 protective factors in Column 1 at −1 each;
- 6 biological/environmental risk factors in Column 2 at +2 each;
- 2 disease indicators in Column 3 at +3 each;
- immediate score and category recalculation whenever a Yes checkbox is
  checked or unchecked;
- the published quantitative bands: Low below 0, Moderate from 0–3, High from
  4–13, and Very High at 14 or greater;
- High guidance when a disease indicator or current/recent household decay is
  documented, plus a separate severe/extensive recent-decay modifier that can
  support Very High guidance;
- a clinician-selected final category that is never changed automatically;
- explicit review when the child encounter's `Caries detected` value conflicts
  with the CAMBRA disease indicator, including an action to reuse a positive
  caries finding in the assessment; and
- structured Combined, Dentist, and Hygienist note output containing Yes
  responses, column totals, total score, final category, and notes.

## Compatibility

Existing `cariesRiskLevel`, `cariesRiskFactors`, and `cariesRiskNotes` values
remain as a legacy read/output path. An older local draft continues to show and
generate its existing caries-risk documentation until the clinician explicitly
starts CAMBRA123. Legacy factors are not mapped automatically because the old
free-text concepts do not have safe one-to-one equivalents in the validated
instrument.

New CAMBRA123 state is additive to the existing local-draft shape. Restored
drafts that predate the new field receive an empty assessment.

The Child Recare Exam & Hygiene draft stores the selected instrument and a
separate assessment for each age band. Restored drafts that predate these
fields receive no selected instrument and empty assessments, while switching
between instruments retains the entered answers for both.

## Ages 0–6 implementation

The ages 0–6 slice is implemented as a separate
`Cambra123ZeroToSixAssessment` and item catalogue behind the shared
caries-assessment boundary. It is integrated with the Child Recare Exam &
Hygiene encounter and does not infer the age band from dentition or silently
switch instruments.
