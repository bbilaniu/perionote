# CAMBRA123 2021 Caries Risk Assessment

- Date: 2026-08-27
- Status: Implemented for ages 6–adult; ages 0–6 deferred
- Clinical acceptance: Pending

## Decision

The 2026 Adult and Adolescent Hygiene encounter uses the January 2021
CAMBRA123 caries-risk assessment for ages 6 through adult. The published age
bands are represented as `0-6` and `6-adult`; dentition state is not part of
instrument selection.

The ages 0–6 assessment remains a separate, deferred implementation. It must
use its own published items, scoring guidance, and `Very High` category rather
than reusing the ages 6–adult `Extreme` category.

## Current implementation

The shared 2026 encounter provides:

- the 8 protective factors in Column 1 at −1 each;
- the 8 biological/environmental risk factors in Column 2 at +2 each;
- the 4 disease indicators in Column 3 at +3 each;
- immediate score and category recalculation whenever a Yes checkbox is
  checked or unchecked;
- the CAMBRA123 quantitative score and published score band;
- guideline flags for disease indicators and measured reduced salivary
  function;
- an explicit clinician-selected final category that is never changed
  automatically; and
- structured note output containing Yes responses, column totals, total score,
  final category, and notes.

The assessment belongs to the shared encounter, so it appears in Complete,
Hygiene, and Recare output and is available in both the 2026 Adult and 2026
Adolescent templates.

## Compatibility

Existing `cariesRiskLevel`, `cariesRiskFactors`, and `cariesRiskNotes` values
remain as a legacy read/output path. An older local draft continues to show and
generate its existing caries-risk documentation until the clinician explicitly
starts CAMBRA123. Legacy factors are not mapped automatically because the old
free-text concepts do not have safe one-to-one equivalents in the validated
instrument.

New CAMBRA123 state is additive to the existing local-draft shape. Restored
drafts that predate the new field receive an empty assessment.

## Source and use

Clinical content and guidance are based on:

- Featherstone et al., *Evidence-Based Caries Management for All
  Ages—Practical Guidelines*, Frontiers in Oral Health, 2021:
  https://oralhealthsupport.ucsf.edu/media/5706
- UCSF CAMBRA methodology and licensing information:
  https://dentistry.ucsf.edu/research/cambra

## Deferred ages 0–6 slice

The future slice should add a separate `Cambra123ZeroToSixAssessment` and item
catalogue behind the same high-level caries-assessment boundary. It should be
integrated with the Child Recare Exam & Hygiene encounter and must not infer
the age band from dentition or silently switch instruments.
