# Topical anesthetic products

Cetacaine liquid is available in the shared Local Anesthesia catalogue and the
imported hygiene webforms under the Topical route. Its label identifies
benzocaine 14%, butamben 2%, and tetracaine HCl 2%.

The existing Mucosal application and Sulcular application choices apply. The
clinician records the application type, treated areas, and actual amount used.
Cetacaine has no preset amount; selecting it also clears any previous product's
amount. The 24 g bottle size is packaging and is not included as an administered
quantity. Catalogue metadata permits products without a default amount and
preserves that absence through export/import.

Topical amounts can be entered in hundredths of a millilitre. Totals retain
small fractional volumes: a synthetic 0.05 mL entry totals 0.05 mL instead of
being rounded to 0.1 mL. Existing whole-volume totals retain one decimal place.

Cetacaine and Oraqix both support non-injectable periodontal application, but
have different formulations and product instructions. No Oraqix amount or
administration protocol is copied to Cetacaine.
